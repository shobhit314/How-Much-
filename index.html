      How we worked this out

      Energy per hour = device power + data used × (network + data-centre energy per GB). Emissions = energy × India’s grid factor (about 0.71 kg CO₂ per kWh) . Making the device is spread over the years you keep it. These are rough, published-range estimates. The real number could be half or double. Every assumption sits at the top of app.js.
